2 · THE LANGUAGE

The AI composes a constrained program.

CONTROLLED EXTENSION IRIllustrative, simplified
on: OrderConfirmed
steps:
  - id: order
    call: orders.get(input.orderId)
  - id: customer
    call: customers.get(order.customerId)
  - id: overLimit
    let: order.total > customer.creditLimit
  - id: hold
    call: orders.hold(order.id)
    when: overLimit
  - id: review
    call: tasks.create(customer.ownerId)
    when: overLimit
the app's operationsthe customer's wiring, in CEL
01Compose app operations

Reads and writes from the app's catalog, in any order the behavior needs.

02Wire them with data

Each step can use earlier results: the order's customer, the customer's owner.

03CEL is the glue

Conditions and data mapping in CEL (Common Expression Language). It computes; it can't act.

04Nothing else

No imports, URLs, loops or free-form code.
